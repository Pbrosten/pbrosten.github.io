Join us in Barcelona on September 10th 2027 to celebrate.

Peter & Elena first met in Seattle in the fall of 2021. Elena had recently begun a year as an au pair, embarking on her first experience living abroad, far from Catalunya. Having just returned from two years in Norway, Peter knew all too well the courage it takes to move to a new country and embrace a different culture.

What began as a friendship soon blossomed into something more. When Elena’s year in Seattle came to an end and moved back to Catalunya, they decided to give a long-distance relationship a try. For the next six months, they made the distance work, until it was no longer necessary. In September 2022, Peter packed his bags and moved to Barcelona.

Four years later, they are still joyfully in love and finally have an answer to the question their friends and family have been asking ever since:

September 10th, 2027. That’s when we’re getting married.
